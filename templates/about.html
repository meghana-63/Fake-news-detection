MISSION & ETHICAL MANIFESTO

About TruthLens AI

Democratizing media literacy through statistical linguistic analysis and real-time news index exploration.

PROJECT PURPOSE

Bridging the Misinformation Divide

In an era saturated with algorithmic content curation, sensationalism, and manufactured narratives, evaluating the credibility of online news has become an urgent societal imperative.

TruthLens AI was designed from first principles as an intelligent analytical workbench for journalists, researchers, educators, and everyday readers. Rather than operating as an authoritative black box that imposes binary "true" or "false" verdicts, TruthLens AI decomposes claims into structural linguistic markers and exposes cross-source reporting from global news indexes.

ETHICAL COMMITMENT

Transparent, Unbiased Verification

A fundamental principle of TruthLens AI is that no machine learning model or search API can act as an arbiter of factual truth.

  • No Unsupported Verdicts: Statistical models classify vocabulary patterns, not real-world reality. We explicitly state when evidence is insufficient.
  • Non-Dogmatic Search Indexing: The lack of search hits does not mean a statement is fabricated; it simply indicates absence from indexed mainstream publishers.
  • Source Attribution: We never generate synthetic news excerpts or fabricate citations. All live articles reference their original publishers with direct links.
TRAINING DATASET CONTEXT

Model Lineage & Training Boundaries

The core classification engine uses a supervised Logistic Regression classifier trained on over 44,000 political and general news articles from the established ISOT News dataset (Fake.csv and True.csv).

While the classifier achieved ~98.9% test accuracy on historical data splits, real-world language evolves continuously. For this reason, TruthLens AI pairs the static model with TheNewsAPI live query integration, ensuring contemporary coverage is always surfaced to complement algorithmic pattern recognition.
