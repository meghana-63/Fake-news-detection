TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE

How TruthLens AI Works

A deep dive into the dual-engine methodology combining NLP feature extraction and real-time news retrieval.

01
PREPROCESSING STAGE

Deterministic Text Normalization

When text is submitted, it immediately passes through a deterministic text normalization pipeline that matches the exact mathematical assumptions of the trained model:

1. Convert text to lowercase.
2. Strip embedded URLs (http://, https://, www.*).
3. Eliminate HTML markup tags (<div>, <p>, etc.).
4. Strip standard ASCII punctuation marks.
5. Filter out numerical digit sequences.
6. Collapse redundant whitespace down to single spaces.
02
VECTORIZATION STAGE

50,000-Dimensional TF-IDF Space

Cleaned text is vectorized using a pre-computed TfidfVectorizer vocabulary. The vectorizer maps text into statistical n-grams (unigrams and bigrams), weighing distinctive vocabulary while down-weighting ubiquitous stop words.

This captures subtle linguistic markers, such as excessive emotional intensifiers, passive-voice ambiguity, and stylistic patterns characteristic of manufactured or sensationalized news writing.

03
CLASSIFICATION STAGE

Logistic Regression & Probability Calibration

The feature vector is passed to the trained Logistic Regression classifier. The model computes the decision boundary log-odds and applies the logistic sigmoid function to generate posterior probabilities for both classes:

Class 0 (Fake News Pattern) Indicates structural affinity with hyper-partisan or fabricated text.
Class 1 (Real News Pattern) Indicates stylistic parity with institutional, edited journalism.

Note: High confidence represents high statistical certainty in pattern matching, never an absolute guarantee of historical truth.

04
RETRIEVAL STAGE

Multi-Tier TheNewsAPI Verification

Simultaneously, the system queries TheNewsAPI. To maximize recall across varying phrasing, TruthLens AI executes a multi-query sequence:

  1. Full Clean Query: Searches using the original headline text to find direct syndicate matches.
  2. Topical Keyword Query: Extracts key named entities and high-entropy keywords to uncover conceptual reporting.
  3. Deduplication: Eliminates duplicate articles across publishers by tracking canonical URLs and normalized titles.
05
SYNTHESIS STAGE

Evidence Alignment & Objective Synthesis

The system computes lexical overlap and Jaccard similarity between the claim and the retrieved news excerpts. Articles are ranked and categorized into Supporting Coverage, Related Context, or Peripheral Mentions.

Finally, TruthLens AI synthesizes the model's pattern classification and the real-time news findings into a transparent dossier, empowering the reader to review original sources and draw their own verified conclusions.
